# TRANSCENDENCE Production Phases

## Phase 0 — Foundation
Repository, deployment, Supabase architecture, shared data model, safe environment configuration, deployment discipline, visual identity rules, asset licensing rules, and the core TRANSCENDENCE art direction (stylized anime 3D, cinematic lighting, teal/cyan System language).

## Phase 1 — First Movement
Opening sequence with minimal text, character choice, first-person identity, initial world, movement, interaction, first NPCs, starter equipment, first mission, basic progression and save state. Character presentation must use designed humanoid silhouettes and layered outfits rather than primitive placeholders.

## Phase 2 — Living 3D World
Rigged humanoid character pipeline, skeletal posing, third-person camera, terrain, vegetation, ruins, lighting, atmosphere, futuristic settlement language, sky/sun/planet presentation, wildlife, multiple NPC archetypes, multiple species visual prototypes, combat enemies, boss presentation, radar, world objective layer, mobile controls and Realtime presence.

## Phase 3 — Combat Core
Authoritative combat model, stamina, hit validation, attack chains, dodge, hit reaction, damage states, enemy archetypes, boss phases, telegraphs, loot and death rules. Combat presentation must preserve character readability, distinctive silhouettes, weapon identity, VFX hierarchy and cinematic impact.

## Phase 4 — Aerion Vertical Slice
A complete beginning-to-end playable loop: arrival → exploration → NPC relationships → investigation → wilderness → dungeon entrance → dungeon rooms → Guardian encounter → permanent consequence → Transfer Point. Aerion receives the first full visual pass for architecture, interiors/exteriors, lighting, flora, fauna, NPC clothing, UI, sky and cinematic presentation.

## Phase 5 — Character & Progression
Attributes, skills, classes/roles, equipment slots, rarity, crafting, titles, reputation, achievements, authority seeds, advancement conditions, Demigod level 999 ceiling. Expand the visual character system with male/female/androgynous body presentation, curated appearance presets, species families, hairstyles, outfits, armor, weapons and readable progression visual changes. Implement the Echo Thread and Trader Thread hooks here where their player-facing identity first becomes meaningful.

## Phase 6 — Multiplayer Foundation
Authoritative simulation service, intent messages, reconciliation, interpolation, reconnect-safe sessions, player identity, parties, shared combat, shared mission state. Introduce the visual multiplayer layer: crowd readability, party silhouettes, nameplates, cooperative VFX, player density management and guild identity. Begin the Guild Thread with player-built faction identity.

## Phase 7 — World Persistence
Server-side characters, inventories, world events, NPC state, quest state, irreversible choices, time progression, faction state, player history. Persist visual/world consequences as well as state: NPC relationships, settlement changes, faction ownership, named-item histories and Shared Board records. Damien Thread remains optional and emergent rather than mandatory.

## Phase 8 — Dungeon & Event Engine
Procedural dungeon generation, team-size gates, rare missions, timed events, world bosses, failure states, branching routes, event scheduling and seeded randomness. Maintain authored visual grammar so procedural content still feels like TRANSCENDENCE rather than generic test geometry.

## Phase 9 — Planetary Scale
Multiple regions, additional planets/worlds, transfer points, vehicles, travel systems, race-specific content, world-specific rules and economies. Each world receives its own signature colour language, architecture, climate, sky, flora/fauna, species presence, NPC culture and settlement variation.

## Phase 10 — Universal Scale
Cross-world travel, universe boundaries, inter-universe powers, god domains, world memory, advanced authorities, rare God+ beings and reality-affecting events. Expand the visual language to planetary scale, orbital environments, moons, stars, nebulae, universe boundaries and distinct cosmic civilizations.

## Phase 11 — Transcendence
Titles, concepts, authority, faith, will, causality, dimensional traversal, reality manipulation and post-level progression. Visual design shifts from conventional gear/status presentation toward aura, authority manifestations, reality distortion, dimensional effects and unmistakable God/Transcendent identity.

## Phase 12 — Unknown
Systems deliberately undisclosed until discovered in-game. Future stages beyond Transcendence, hidden dimensions, unknown authorities and emergent player-discovered mechanics. Their visuals must remain deliberately difficult to predict until revealed through play.

## Build rule
Every phase must produce a playable increase in capability. The project should never be represented as complete merely because menus or placeholder systems exist. Visual placeholders are temporary only; before a phase is accepted, its player-facing art must meet the current TRANSCENDENCE visual language and must not regress to primitive capsule/ball presentation.
