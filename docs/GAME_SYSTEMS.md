# TRANSCENDENCE Game Systems Contract

## Identity

**Name:** TRANSCENDENCE
**Genre:** multiplayer 3D action RPG combining fighting, survival/horror, strategy, adventure and mission-driven progression.
**Core theme:** self-improvement through adversity, choices, consequences, discovery and evolution.
**Narrative protagonist:** Damien Dane.

## Player fantasy

A player begins as a comparatively ordinary being in a vast multiverse and can develop through many possible roles. The system is a tool, not the entire game. Character identity, choices, relationships, knowledge and consequences matter as much as numerical power.

## Universe model

The game is not Earth-centered. A player may eventually start or be assigned to different planets, worlds, universes and multiverse branches. Intelligent species have different cultures, histories, abilities and progression conditions. NPCs and world events are partially randomized within authored rules.

## Progression

The progression has many stages before Godhood. Exact production naming can evolve, but the structural contract is:

Mortal → Enhanced → Awakened → Evolved → Ascended → Exalted → Saint → Demigod → God → higher divine/cosmic stages → Transcendent → Unknown.

Demigod can progress to level 999. God and higher do not depend on ordinary numeric levels. Each God-and-above stage has three meaningful named ranks rather than generic Rank I/II/III labels. The highest or hidden states may use unknown titles that are not visible to ordinary players.

## Godhood and beyond

Gods can traverse space and, depending on rank/authority, worlds and universes. Gods can be killed, but killing a god from much lower stages is near-impossible. Gods can also become lost, sealed or displaced through exploration and time-related phenomena.

Transcendent entities can affect reality, fate and dimensional structures. Transcendence is not a final conventional level and is not assumed to be populated by many identical beings. Higher existence becomes rarer and increasingly title/authority driven.

## System visibility

Low-level characters may not see a complete interface. Higher beings may deliberately suppress, transcend or replace conventional system information. Presence, authority and observable effects can communicate power without a numeric UI.

## Missions

Missions can originate from normal quest channels, hidden systems, NPCs, factions, world events, dungeons, discoveries or inappropriate/unexpected sources. Missions can alter a player's life, unlock or close paths, and become permanently unavailable after failure. Some rare missions are elevation/decision missions with major consequences.

High-intelligence bosses, gods and other powerful entities can delay, interrupt, obstruct or reroute a player's journey. The player may need to take a long alternate route or accept a different future.

## Dungeons and halls

Supported dungeon categories include solo, level-restricted, party-sized, large-scale team, hidden, random and world-event dungeons. Halls and special locations can provide benefits, hazards, information, traps, allies, enemies, rare items or unexpected outcomes. Outcomes should be systemic rather than purely scripted.

## Combat

Combat will support melee, ranged and modern weapons, abilities, resources, enemy behaviors, environmental interactions and boss mechanics. Server authority must validate consequential multiplayer combat actions.

## Multiplayer roles

Damien is the designed narrative hero but not every player is required to be him or to become a hero. Players can become support characters, explorers, strategists, merchants, faction leaders, protectors, villains, mercenaries, rulers or other roles. Multiple heroes can exist. A player can survive without reaching Transcendence.

## Social systems

Friends, parties, factions, alliances, rivalries, betrayal, reputation, trust and relationships are first-class systems. A special system-linked companion/undroppable item is planned for a later narrative milestone.

## World events

Rare and common events can occur dynamically. Some require many players to cooperate. Others can create emergencies, wars, disasters, invasions, discoveries, opportunities or permanent historical changes.

## Travel

Transfer Points become discoverable during mid-stage progression rather than being locked to Godhood. Later, players can travel through space using vehicles/crafts and eventually through space under their own power at sufficiently high stages.

## Economy

Currencies, transfer/travel resources and other economic instruments will be introduced progressively. The economy must be authoritative server-side and auditable.

## First playable slice

The initial slice deliberately proves the loop before scale:

create character → enter world → explore → interact → receive mission → make choice → experience consequence → gain progression → save.

The first slice is a technical foundation, not the final visual quality or content scope.
