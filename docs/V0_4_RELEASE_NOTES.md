# v0.4 Release Notes

The prototype now has a clearer playable loop around exploration, combat, consequence and world transfer.

## Player loop
1. Choose a race.
2. Enter Aerion.
3. Explore points of interest and speak to NPCs.
4. Fight roaming threats and earn XP.
5. Trigger a rare decision.
6. Discover a Transfer Point.
7. Enter Velis Depths.
8. Return to Aerion with a changed state.

## Systems introduced
- Race selection
- Persistent local character state
- Level and attribute progression
- Authority and title progression
- Dynamic world time and weather labels
- Combat and enemy pursuit
- Guardian boss encounter
- Transfer between two worlds
- Permanent-loss decision state
- Mobile touch movement and attack
- Supabase Realtime presence
- Research-backed multiplayer architecture direction
